💸 Refund Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

We want you to be happy with your project. This policy explains when and how you can get your money back.

🛡️ Our promise: if we do not deliver the quality you were promised, or we do not fulfill your agreed requirements, we will return your amount as soon as possible.

1. When You Get a Refund

  • We do not deliver the work you agreed on with us.
  • The final result does not meet the requirements agreed in writing, even after your included revisions (2 to 3) have been used.
  • We are unable to complete the project for any reason on our side.

In these cases, we return the amount you paid to us.

2. How It Works

  • Tell us what is not right on WhatsApp or email, with details of what was agreed.
  • We first try to fix the issue through your included revisions.
  • If we still cannot meet the agreed requirements, we confirm your refund and send the money back as soon as possible using the same payment method where we can.

3. Payment Structure

Projects are paid in two parts: 50% advance to start and 50% after full work is completed. If we have not completed the work, you are not asked to pay the second 50%.

4. When a Refund May Not Apply

  • The request is for changes outside the original agreed scope.
  • Delays were caused by missing content, files or feedback from the client side.
  • Fees paid directly to third parties (domains, hosting, Shopify plans, apps, themes) which we cannot recover.

5. Fast Delivery Charges

If we fail to deliver the work you paid for, any extra fast-delivery charge you paid for that work is also included in your refund.
